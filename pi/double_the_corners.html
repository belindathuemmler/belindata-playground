Double the Corners

Now double the corners: 12, 24, 48, 96. With every doubling the inner polygon grows and the outer one shrinks, and \(\pi\) stays in between. Each new side length follows from the old one by Pythagoras, nothing but square roots. Archimedes had no decimals, so he replaced every root by a fraction, always on the safe side.
