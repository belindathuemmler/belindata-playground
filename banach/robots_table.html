The Robot’s Table

The corridor, the station in cell 5, and the whole table \(Q\): four cells times two actions. The table starts with random numbers, the way a network does. Tap any entry to see its own line of the equation with today’s numbers, then apply one round of \(T\): every entry is replaced by its right-hand side. Watch the largest change per round and the distance to the true table \(Q^*\) shrink by the factor \(\gamma = 0.8\), and watch the arrows under the corridor settle on the way to the station. At any moment, let the robot walk: it follows the arrows of the table as it stands. A random table usually sends it the wrong way; watch how few rounds it takes until the arrows are right, long before the numbers are.
