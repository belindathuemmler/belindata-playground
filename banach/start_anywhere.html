Start Anywhere

The graph of \(f(x) = L \cdot x + 2\,(1 - L)\) is a line that crosses the diagonal \(y = x\) at \((2, 2)\): that crossing is the fixed point, for every \(L\). With \(L = 0.5\) it is the \(x/2 + 1\) from above. Click anywhere to pick a start on the \(x\)-axis, then step: up to the graph, across to the diagonal, up to the graph again. Every move is \(L\) times the last. Then push \(L\) past \(1\) and see what the theorem no longer promises.
