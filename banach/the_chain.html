The Chain, Row by Row

The rule is \(f(x) = \tfrac{x}{2} + 1\), which means: go halfway to \(2\). The top row holds two starting points \(x\) and \(y\); every row below applies \(f\) once more to both. Drag \(x\) and \(y\) anywhere, or roll a random start, and watch the distance between them halve in every row while both columns walk to \(2\).
