Halfway to Two

The rule \(f(x) = \tfrac{x}{2} + 1\) has a plainer name: go halfway to 2, because \(\tfrac{x}{2} + 1\) is the midpoint between \(x\) and \(2\). Two walkers start at \(10\) and \(6\). Step, and watch two things: every hop is half the one before, and the distance between the walkers halves as well. Drag the walkers anywhere on the line, click the line to place them, or roll a random start.
