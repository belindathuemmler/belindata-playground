Interactive

The Parking Lot

Your car has two controls, steer and drive, and no third one for sideways. The gap at the kerb is right below you. Try to get in by hand, or let the prepared move sequences show you what the order of moves leaves behind.

The Bracket Fields

Each button spells a bracket out as moves and plays them one by one: X is one click of steering, Y one click of driving, a minus sign undoes it. At the end the start (pale) and the end (solid) are compared, and what is left between them is the bracket field.
