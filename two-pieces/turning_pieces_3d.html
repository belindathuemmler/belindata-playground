Which Two Build the Block?

The same game with cubes: four pieces, and exactly two of them build the 3 × 2 × 2 block. Drag a piece onto the block; turn it with the buttons. It snaps to the grid. When the block is filled exactly, you have it. Swipe across the empty floor to look at everything from another side.
