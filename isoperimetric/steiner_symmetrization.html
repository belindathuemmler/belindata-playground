Steiner’s Move: Same Area, Less Fence

An odd-shaped field with a fixed area of 25.78 m². Draw a line across it with your mouse or finger: that is your axis. Then symmetrize: every strip perpendicular to the axis slides until its midpoint lies on the axis. The area never changes, but its shape does, and thus the fence gets shorter. Repeat in other directions and watch the field turn into a circle.
