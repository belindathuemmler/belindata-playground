Steiner’s Move: Same Area, Less Fence

An odd-shaped field. Draw a line across it with your mouse or finger: that is your axis. Then symmetrize: every strip perpendicular to the axis slides until its midpoint lies on the axis. The area never changes, the fence only gets shorter. Repeat in other directions and watch the field turn into a circle.
