The Dark Room

Two identical dark rooms, two ways of exploring them. Both come in through the door. Wherever an explorer spends time, the room lights up. Which one finds everything first?

Fair race: both walk at the same speed and leave the same amount of light. Seconds = how long you watch. Steps = decisions: a jump tried by the flashlight (taken or not), a turn of the string of lights.

🔦 The flashlight
Metropolis–Hastings

Jumps to a random spot nearby. If it looks more promising there, it stays; if not, it stays only sometimes and otherwise jumps back.

✨ The string of lights
Zig-Zag

Walks in straight lines and changes direction at random moments, more often when it is heading somewhere less promising.
